Delete your Lifecare Companion account

Lifecare Companion™ is made by Jan-Christian Zaluski, trading as Companion Apps Studio™ (the data controller).

Delete it yourself (quickest)

  1. Open Lifecare Companion: the Android app, or the web app at jcbeck1976.github.io/Life-Care on any phone or computer.
  2. Sign in with the same Google account.
  3. Tap ⚙ Settings, then go to Privacy & your data.
  4. Optional: tap Download my data to keep a copy.
  5. Under Delete my account, type DELETE and tap Delete my account.

Your records are erased straight away and you are signed out. Your Google account itself is not affected.

Can't sign in? Ask us to delete it

Email the developer contact address shown on the app's Google Play page (“Developer contact” → Email), from the Google account you used, with the subject Delete my Lifecare Companion account. We confirm and delete within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your sign-in to Lifecare Companion and everything recorded for you — profile, goals, body stats and measurements, exercise logs, eating lists and logs, health notes, mood and journal, Companion feedback and chats, and photo-check results (photos themselves are never stored).
  • Kept for a limited time: encrypted backups age out and are deleted within 12 months; your deleted data disappears from them as they do. A record that a deletion happened (date and action only, no content) is kept in the audit trail.
